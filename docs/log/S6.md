## Decisions
- **`affarsplan`, `affarsplan-exempel` and `affarsplan-mall` use the slugs named in plan §6.3**, marked `legacy: true` on the two articles, since `content/legacy-urls.json` still lists them only under `todo_unknown_slugs` (file is `"complete": false`). Logged in `docs/decisions-needed.md`.
- **Body links stay strictly to hub and tool paths, with no exceptions for same-phase content** — `affarsplan` does not link to `affarsplan-exempel` or to the `affarsplan-mall` lead magnet even though S6 wrote all three, per the common rule that cross-content links are the link pass's job. S9 wires these.
- **`affarsideer-liten-budget` and `affarsideer-vid-sidan-av-jobbet` are guidance-first, not re-lists** — each carries a distinct practical angle (zero-budget playbook; bisyssla/employment-contract rules) with its own curated ~15-item list, kept mostly non-overlapping with the cornerstone's 56 to avoid cannibalising its intent.
- **Lead magnets ship as plain Markdown with a proposed `gate: newsletter` frontmatter field** — no MDX components, since they render outside the article template once a page-like loader exists for them.
