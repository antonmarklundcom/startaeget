## Decisions
- `enskild-firma-eller-aktiebolag` ships as `type: guide` with a plain Markdown table, not `type: comparison` — the comparison schema's columns (price/freeTier) are shaped for product comparisons (software, banks, insurers), not a two/three-way company-form decision, and `content/comparisons/**` isn't in S4's Owns block.
- Legacy slugs for the "enskild firma", "bidrag" and "företagslån" WordPress posts were invented (`enskild-firma`, `stod-till-nytt-foretag`, `foretagslan`) since `content/legacy-urls.json` is still `"complete": false` and lists exactly these three as unresolved in `todo_unknown_slugs`, per the phase prompt's fallback rule.
- `stod-till-nytt-foretag` (general support overview) and `starta-eget-bidrag` (Arbetsförmedlingen's specific scheme) were kept as two separate articles with distinct intents to avoid cannibalisation, cross-linking each other.
- Fee/rate figures matched `src/lib/tax/constants.ts` exactly (2 200 kr AB e-tjänst registration, 1 200 kr enskild/handelsbolag, 28,97 % egenavgifter, moms 25/12/6 %) and are cited with a source link, `<Verifiera />` only where O3's own constant is itself a recent, contested change (the AB fee) or where a reader-specific figure can't be stated as one number (the Arbetsförmedlingen grant amount/duration, the moms omsättningsgräns threshold).
