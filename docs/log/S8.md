- **`google-foretagsprofil` and `marknadsforing-nyforetagare` are `legacy: false`** — genuinely new pages, not on the old WordPress site.
- **`/redovisningsbyra/`** is the only page with `leadForm: true`; the other six are informational/legal and render without the byrå form.
- **`/nyhetsbrev/`** links to the two lead magnets S6 already wrote (`/affarsplan-mall/`, `/startkostnads-checklista/`); both still 404 until B1 ships the lead-magnet loader (already logged by S6 in `docs/decisions-needed.md`) — verify reports these as warnings, as designed.
